## Vision

SwarmAI is a live experiment proving that one builder + AI can operate at team scale.
Human directs, AI delivers — coding, content, strategy, and operations through
self-evolving systems and compound knowledge.

Human directs. AI delivers.

## What Makes SwarmAI Different

Most AI assistants are stateless -- every conversation starts from scratch. SwarmAI is
built around **persistent context** and a **self-growing intelligence core**: value
compounds across sessions instead of resetting.

- **Memory that persists** -- a layered pipeline (raw activity → distillation → curated
  memory) with git-verified accuracy. Decisions, lessons, and preferences survive across
  sessions rather than being re-explained.
- **Knowledge that accumulates** -- each project is a DDD (domain brain). Knowledge
  sediments in as work happens and decays when it stops mattering, so what reaches
  judgment stays true and load-bearing.
- **Skills that compound** -- a library of invocable capabilities; new ones are built
  when a capability gap is hit, and they improve from use.
- **An autonomous pipeline** -- one requirement → push-ready delivery as a black box,
  guarded by adversarial quality gates rather than by iterating cheap drafts.
- **Self-evolution** -- recurring mistakes become structural gates where the wrong move
  can't happen, not one more logged lesson.

## The DDD Judgment Loop

A DDD exists to make the agent **judge better**, not to store documents. The four
knowledge docs answer the four questions every task faces:

| Question | Doc |
|----------|-----|
| **Should we?** (priorities, non-goals) | PRODUCT.md |
| **Can we?** (architecture, constraints) | TECH.md |
| **Have we tried?** (what worked / failed) | IMPROVEMENT.md |
| **What now?** (current focus) | PROJECT.md |

## Strategic Priorities

1. **Compound intelligence** -- memory, knowledge cultivation, and self-evolution are the
   moat. Every unit of work should leave the system sharper than before.
2. **Core stability** -- multi-session architecture, resource management, streaming
   reliability. Nothing else matters if the foundation isn't solid.
3. **User experience** -- fast iteration, clear errors, intuitive UX. The tool should
   disappear into the workflow.
4. **Autonomy progression** -- from AI-Assistant to AI-Driven to AI-Management. Gradual
   trust-building, not a cliff.

## Success Criteria

- Sessions never crash or lose context unexpectedly.
- The agent remembers decisions and lessons across sessions without being told.
- New skills can be created when a capability gap appears.
- Users spend more time doing, less time re-explaining.
- Context compounds: a late session is meaningfully more productive than an early one.

## Non-Goals

- **Not a cloud SaaS** -- desktop-first, local-first. Your data stays on your machine.
- **Not a general chatbot** -- opinionated, workspace-scoped. Built for people who ship.
- **Not code-only** -- handles research, writing, communication, planning. A full
  teammate, not a coding copilot.
- **Not a framework** -- SwarmAI is a product. It has opinions about how AI assistance
  should work.

## Target Users

Knowledge workers, developers, and leaders who want an AI teammate, not an AI tool —
people who work across multiple projects, value accumulated context, and prefer action
over conversation.

## Autonomy Phases

| Phase | Name | Essence | Human Role |
|-------|------|---------|-----------|
| 1 | AI-Assistant | Human decides, AI executes | Direct every task |
| 2 | AI-Driven | AI proposes, human approves | Review and approve |
| 3 | AI-Management | AI decides + evolves, human triages | Intervene when needed |
